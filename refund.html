Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At KnitKnotKart, we strive to ensure our customers are completely satisfied with their purchases. Please read our policy regarding cancellations, returns, and refunds carefully.

1. Order Cancellation

  • Standard Products: You can request a cancellation within 24 hours of placing your order. Once the item has been packed or shipped, cancellations cannot be processed.
  • Custom Orders: Because custom crochet plushies are made specific to your design/requirements, custom orders cannot be cancelled once production has started.

2. Returns & Replacements

Since our physical plushie toys are 100% handmade, we do not accept general returns or exchanges based on personal preference. However, your item is eligible for a replacement or return only if:

  • The product received is physically damaged during transit.
  • The wrong item or color variant was delivered to you.

Note: To claim a replacement for damage, an unboxing video showing the damage clearly is highly recommended. You must report the issue within 48 hours of delivery by emailing us at knitknotkart@gmail.com.

3. Digital Products (Crochet Patterns)

All sales of digital products, including PDF crochet patterns, are final. Since digital files are delivered instantly upon successful payment, they are non-refundable and non-returnable under any circumstances.

4. Refund Process & Timeline

Once your return/refund request is approved by our team, the refund will be processed back to your original payment method via our payment gateway (Razorpay).

  • The approved refund amount will be credited to your account within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank's processing cycles.

5. Contact Us

For any issues related to returns, cancellations, or refunds, please reach out to us at knitknotkart@gmail.com with your order details.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Thank you for shopping at KnitKnotKart. Since our physical products (Plushie Toys) are meticulously handmade with love, our shipping timelines include both production/processing time and transit time.

1. Shipping Locations

We currently ship physical products PAN India across all major pin codes. Digital products (Crochet Patterns) are available globally via instant download upon successful payment confirmation.

2. Processing & Making Time

  • Ready-made Items: Standard plushies that are already in stock are processed and dispatched within 2 to 3 working days.
  • Custom/Made-to-Order Items: Since custom orders require dedicated manual crafting, they can take anywhere between 5 to 10 working days to be prepared before dispatch, depending on the complexity.

3. Delivery Timelines (Transit Time)

Once your order is handed over to our trusted courier partners, the estimated delivery timelines are as follows:

  • Metro Cities: 3 to 5 working days.
  • Rest of India: 5 to 7 working days.

Note: Total time to reach your doorstep = Processing/Making Time + Delivery Transit Time. We will share a tracking link/ID via email/SMS once the order is shipped.

4. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated based on the weight, dimensions, and delivery location of the items. Any applicable shipping fees will be clearly displayed at the checkout page before you make the final payment.

5. Delivery Delays

While we ensure timely dispatch from our end, delivery timelines might occasionally face minor delays due to public holidays, extreme weather conditions, or courier partner issues. We appreciate your patience in such situations.

6. Contact Us

If you have any queries regarding your order tracking or shipping details, feel free to reach out to us at knitknotkart@gmail.com.
